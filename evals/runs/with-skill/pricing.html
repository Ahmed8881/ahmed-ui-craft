Questions before you start

What happens when the 14-day trial ends?

Your workspace turns read-only. Nothing is deleted and we never ask for a card up front. Pick a plan whenever you are ready and editing comes back straight away.

What counts as a client?

A client is any customer you invoiced or logged time against in the current month. Archived clients with no activity do not count toward your limit.

Can I change plans or switch between monthly and annual?

Yes, at any time. Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated. Downgrades and billing-period changes take effect at your next renewal.

Which banks can I connect?

Ledgerly connects to most banks and card issuers in the US, UK and EU through read-only bank feeds. We can see transactions but cannot move money. If your bank is missing, you can import statements as CSV.

Does Ledgerly handle sales tax and VAT?

It tracks tax collected and paid on every invoice and expense, and produces a summary you can hand to your accountant or file yourself. It does not file returns on your behalf.

Can I take my data with me if I leave?

Yes. Export invoices, expenses and the full transaction ledger as CSV from any plan, including during the trial and after you cancel, for 90 days.
